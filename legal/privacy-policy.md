# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 12, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how FitnessApp ("the App," "we," "us") collects,
uses, and protects your information. FitnessApp is a working title — if the
app is renamed before release, replace every instance of "FitnessApp" below
with the final name.

## Information We Collect

**Account information:** email address, and full name if you choose to
provide one.

**Health and fitness data you enter:** height, weight, age, sex, fitness
goals, and activity level; workout logs (exercise, sets, reps, duration,
calories); cardio session logs; water intake; body-weight entries over time;
meal and calorie/macro logs, including any custom meals you save.

**Photos:** an optional photo you attach to a workout session. This is
stored in a private location and is never shared publicly or with other
users.

**Basic usage data inherent to the App's operation**, such as the timestamps
of your own log entries — used only to power your own dashboard, streaks,
and rank/XP calculations.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or browsing history, and this
version of the App contains no advertising or third-party analytics
trackers.

## How We Use Your Information

- To operate the App's core features: tracking your workouts, nutrition,
  weight, and progress over time, and calculating your rank/XP.
- To calculate personalized calorie and macro targets from the data you
  provide.
- To power the opt-in Leaderboard, which shares only your display name and
  a single aggregate stat (your level, or a session count) with other
  users — never your weight, age, goals, notes, or photos.
- To respond to support requests you send us.

## How We Store and Protect Your Data

- Your data is stored with Supabase, our backend infrastructure provider,
  behind row-level security rules that restrict access so that only you —
  or a small number of narrowly-scoped functions we control, used solely
  to compute the leaderboard aggregates described above — can read it.
- Your login session is stored using your device's secure, encrypted
  storage, not as plain text.
- Workout photos are kept in a private storage bucket and are only ever
  made viewable to you through short-lived, expiring links.

## Your Choices and Rights

- You can edit or delete any individual log entry at any time within the
  App.
- You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any
  time from **Settings → Delete Account**. This action is immediate and
  cannot be undone.
- You can contact us at any time with a question, or a request to access
  or correct your data, at the email below.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with Supabase, our
infrastructure provider, and only as necessary to operate the App on our
behalf.

## Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete
your account, your data is deleted immediately and permanently, with the
exception of anything we're legally required to retain.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated"
date above reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be
noted in the App.
