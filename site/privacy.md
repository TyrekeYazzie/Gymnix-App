# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 3, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how FitnessApp ("the App," "we," "us") collects,
uses, and protects your information.

## Information We Collect

**Account information:** email address, and full name if you choose to
provide one.

**Health and fitness data you enter:** height, weight, age, sex, fitness
goals, and activity level; workout logs (exercise, sets, reps, duration,
calories); cardio session logs; water intake; body-weight entries over time;
meal and calorie/macro logs, including any custom meals you save.

**Photos:**
- *Workout photos* — an optional photo you attach to a workout session. These
  are stored privately and are never shared with other users.
- *Profile photo* — an optional picture you add to your profile. Unlike
  workout photos, your profile photo is **visible to other users** (next to
  your name on the leaderboards) and can be viewed by anyone who has its
  link. Before upload, the App crops and resizes it and removes embedded
  metadata such as the photo's GPS location and device details. You can
  remove or replace it at any time from your Profile.

**Basic usage data inherent to the App's operation**, such as the timestamps
of your own log entries — used only to power your own dashboard, streaks,
and rank/XP calculations.

**Crash and performance reports:** if the App crashes or hits an error, a
diagnostic report (error details, device model, operating system and App
version, and the screens involved) may be sent to our crash-reporting
provider, Sentry. These reports do not include your name, email address, IP
address, or login tokens, and web addresses are stripped of their query
details before sending.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or browsing history, and the App
contains no advertising, no ad tracking, and no marketing analytics.

## How We Use Your Information

- To operate the App's core features: tracking your workouts, nutrition,
  weight, and progress over time, and calculating your rank/XP.
- To calculate personalized calorie and macro targets from the data you
  provide.
- To power the Leaderboards. **Every account appears on the leaderboards**,
  which show other users your username, your profile photo (if you've added
  one), and a single aggregate stat for that board — your level/XP, your
  number of strength or cardio sessions logged, or your current workout
  streak. Your display name is shown there only to you and to friends you've
  accepted. The leaderboards never show your weight, age, goals, notes,
  meals, or workout photos. If you'd rather not be recognizable, choose a
  username that doesn't identify you and leave your profile photo empty.
- To power Friends. You choose a unique username, which other users can
  search for to send you a friend request; search results show your username
  and profile photo, not your display name. Friend requests need your
  approval. Once you accept, that friend can see your username, display
  name, profile photo, level/XP, current workout streak, number of workouts
  and cardio sessions logged, and which achievements you've unlocked — never
  your weight, age, goals, notes, meals, or workout photos. You can remove a
  friend at any time.
- To keep the leaderboards safe. If you report another user, we store the
  report (who you reported, the reason you picked, and the name or username
  and profile photo shown at that moment) so we can review it; the reported user is
  never told who reported them. If you block someone, we store that so you
  and they no longer see each other on the leaderboards.
- To diagnose crashes and errors and improve the App's reliability.
- To respond to support requests you send us.

## How We Store and Protect Your Data

- Your data is stored with Supabase, our backend infrastructure provider,
  behind row-level security rules that restrict access so that only you —
  or a small number of narrowly-scoped functions we control, used solely
  to compute the leaderboard aggregates described above — can read it.
- Your login session is stored using your device's secure, encrypted
  storage, not as plain text.
- Workout photos are kept in a private storage bucket and are only ever
  made viewable to you through short-lived, expiring links.
- Profile photos are kept in a separate storage bucket that is publicly
  readable (so other users can see them), but only you can upload, replace,
  or delete yours. Deleting your account deletes your profile photo.

## Your Choices and Rights

- You can edit or delete any individual log entry at any time within the
  App.
- You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any
  time from **Settings → Delete Account**. This action is immediate and
  cannot be undone.
- You can contact us at any time with a question, or a request to access
  or correct your data, at the email below.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with these service
providers, and only as necessary to operate the App on our behalf:

- **Supabase** — database, authentication, and file storage.
- **Sentry** — crash and error reporting, as described above.
- **Google (Gmail)** — sends account emails, such as password-reset links,
  and so receives your email address.

Other users can see only what's described in the Leaderboards and Friends
sections above.

## Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete
your account, your data is deleted immediately and permanently, with the
exception of anything we're legally required to retain. Reports you filed
about other users are kept (without your identity attached) so we can
finish reviewing them.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated"
date above reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be
noted in the App.
